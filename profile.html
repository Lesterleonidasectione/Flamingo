John Anderson

Senior Officer

Biography

John Anderson has been a dedicated member of the Lodger's Assembly for over 15 years. With a background in structural engineering, he brings a unique perspective on organization and precision. As a Senior Officer, John plays a vital role in mentoring new members and helping shape the strategic direction of our assembly.

In his spare time, he is a passionate restorer of vintage motorcycles and enjoys hiking in the Ontario backcountry.
